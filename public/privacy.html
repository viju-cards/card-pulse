Privacy
Policy.

Last updated: May 2026

CardPulse does not collect personal data beyond what is necessary for the service to function. We do not sell data to third parties.

1. Who we are

CardPulse is a service that displays TCGPlayer prices on cardmarket.com. Available at card-pulse.com.

2. Data we collect

We collect only the following data required to operate the service:

  • Email address – to create and manage your account
  • Password (hashed) – for secure authentication (never stored in plain text)
  • Payment data – processed exclusively by Stripe; we store no credit card data
  • Stripe Customer ID – to link your account to your subscription
  • Subscription status & period – to verify whether your account has premium access

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not track which cards you view on Cardmarket
  • We do not store search queries or browsing data
  • We do not use third-party advertising trackers or analytics services
  • We do not share data with third parties, except Stripe for payment processing

4. How the browser extension works

The CardPulse browser extension:

  • Reads the page title on Cardmarket.com to identify the set abbreviation and card number
  • Sends only this anonymous card data (e.g. "sv08 / 067") to our server
  • Stores your JWT token locally in Chrome Extension Storage
  • Does not access any other tabs, cookies or personal browsing data

5. Cookies & local storage

The website stores your login token in your browser's localStorage to keep you signed in. You can sign out at any time, which deletes the token.

6. Third parties

  • Stripe – Payment processing. Privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy
  • Neon (PostgreSQL) – Database hosting in the EU
  • Render – Server hosting
  • JustTCG API – Price data source (we only send the card ID, no personal data)

7. Data security

All connections between the extension, website and server are SSL/TLS encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text. JWT tokens expire after 30 days.

8. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, delete, and port your data. For all requests: privacy@card-pulse.com

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@card-pulse.com
